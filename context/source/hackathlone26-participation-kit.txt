                                                           WHAT IS SPACE APPS?
                                                           The NASA International Space Apps Challenge (or Space Apps) is an
                                                           international hackathon that will take place in person over a           48 - hour
                                                           period around the globe on dates        13 th , 14 th and 15 th Nov 2026.

                                                           Since its inception in 2012, NASA’s International Space Apps Challenge
                                                           has engaged 487,000+ registrants           from 190+ countries in using
                                                           NASA’s open data to build innovative solutions that solve challenges
                                                           we face on Earth and in Space.

                                                           The event embraces collaborative problem solving with a goal of
                                                           producing open - source solutions     to challenges we currently face on
                                                           Earth and in space. Space Apps is     open to the public and free for all
                                                           ages to attend.

                                                           Details on this year’s event can be found at
                                                           https://spaceappschallenge.org
