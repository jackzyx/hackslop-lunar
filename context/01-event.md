# HackAthlone 4.0: the event

HackAthlone is the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge local event in Athlone**,
run by TUS (Technological University of the Shannon) and GDG Athlone. 2026 is the
4th edition and the only Space Apps location in Ireland. Local lead: Sahir Sharma
(PhD student in Extended Reality, TUS). Contact: **spaceappsathlone@gmail.com**.
Web: https://hackathlone.com. Sources: participation kit (`source/`), hackathlone.com
home, FAQ, challenges, projects and about pages, GDG Athlone event page; all read 13 Nov 2026.

## Venue

Engineering Building, TUS Athlone, Bunnavally, Athlone, Co. Westmeath, **N37 HD68**
(53.41795, -7.90519). About 3 km from Athlone station: taxi, or A1/A2 town bus;
ATH1 bus from Golden Island. Parking behind the Engineering Building is limited;
email the team to reserve a space. The building stays open all weekend; no
accommodation, no storage. Bring a sleeping bag if staying over.

Nearby (from the kit): SPAR (closest, closes 22:00), Lidl (22:00), **Centra 24/7**,
Chicken Cloud (to 00:00), Lin Asian Street Food (to 01:00), The Good Cafe (09-16),
The Tack Room (college bar).

## Support available

- **Hackshop:** Uno, Raspberry boards, sensors (IR, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, IMU, camera,
  humidity, ultrasonic, gas, sound, LCD, pressure, piezo, NFC, RFID), motors
  (stepper, DC, vibration, buzzer), 9 V battery, jumpers, splitters, connectors,
  resistors, diodes, capacitors, breadboard, PCB boards, LEDs, relays.
- **Labs:** soldering stations, mechanical workshop, screwdrivers, vernier callipers,
  glue gun station, wire cutter, potentiometers, multimeter, AR/VR devices, Google
  Cardboard, whiteboards.
- **Makerspace:** "full of 3D printers" (models, slicer, queue rules unknown: ask).
- **Mentors** from industry and academia, three slots on Saturday plus Sunday dry run.
- Not listed: light sensors, USB webcam, torch, CNC, laser cutter.

## Rules we know

- Teams up to 6. Under-18s registered and accompanied by a guardian.
- 2026 Participant Terms (updated 7 Aug 2026): original content must be open source
  and every source cited. Nothing on when work may begin.
- Space Apps judging criteria: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation
  (see `02-challenge.md`).
- Video: 30 s maximum in past years (export at 29 s). Slides: 7 maximum in past
  years. Re-check against the 2026 Project Submission Guide (released 13 Nov).
